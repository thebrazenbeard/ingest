# Contributing

Contributions are welcome only when the contributor has the legal right to submit them and agrees to the repository contribution terms.

Before submitting a pull request, read `CLA.md` and include: `I have read and agree to the repository CLA.`

Do not submit third-party code, copied material, model output, datasets, or other content unless you have the right to do so and clearly disclose license/attribution obligations. Provenance that cannot be verified is sufficient reason to reject a contribution.
